What can't I do with Poliigon assets? We spend a great deal of time, money and effort building our assets, so there are a number of restrictions on their use: You can't share, sell, license, redistribute or repackage our assets with anyone not named on your account even if they've been modified, directly or indirectly. This includes baked textures This includes providing scene files to clients/others with Poliigon assets in a whole or easily extractable state This includes sharing assets with others in your team/company if they're not listed as a user on your account. You can't use our assets in design software or games where the users have direct or indirect access to the assets for uses such as designing rooms, buildings, levels or scenes. You can't use our assets in any form of Machine Learning (ML) or Artificial Intelligence (AI) models, neural networks or applications. You can't use our assets to create a logo, trademark or copyright of any kind. If you're unsure if you're use case is permitted or you would like to license Poliigon assets for any of the use cases above please contact us at support@poliigon.com . For ML/AI use you can also fill out the form at poliigon.com/ai Additional FAQ Can I use Poliigon assets even after I cancel my subscription? Yes, you can continue to use Poliigon assets even if you cancel your subscription but the above license conditions still apply.
